🌿 Yakinaran Kenda
(Herbal porridge made with Yakinaran / Gynura procumbens)

📝 Ingredients (Serves 3–4)
Fresh Yakinaran leaves – 1 cup (tender young leaves, washed)

Raw rice (red or white) – 1/4 cup

Grated coconut – 1/2 cup (or thick coconut milk – 1 cup)

Garlic – 2–3 cloves

Shallots (small onions) – 2, sliced

Cumin seeds – 1/2 tsp

Fenugreek seeds – 1/4 tsp (optional)

Water – 3–4 cups

Salt – to taste

🍲 Instructions
Cook the rice base:

Rinse and soak rice for about 20–30 minutes.

In a pot, combine the rice, garlic, shallots, cumin, fenugreek, and 3 cups water.

Boil until the rice is very soft and water turns milky-starchy.

Prepare Yakinaran:

Option A (traditional): Finely chop the leaves and add them directly.

Option B (smooth version): Blend Yakinaran leaves with 1/2 cup of water and strain to extract juice.

Add Yakinaran to the porridge:

If chopped: Add leaves once rice is cooked and simmer for 5–7 minutes.

If juice: Turn off the heat and stir in the Yakinaran extract gently to preserve medicinal value.

Add coconut milk:

Add thick coconut milk and warm gently for 2–3 minutes. Do not boil after adding it.

Season:

Add salt to taste.

Serve warm, optionally with:

A piece of jaggery

A banana
