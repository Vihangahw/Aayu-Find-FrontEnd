🌿 Karapincha Kenda Recipe (Curry Leaf Herbal Porridge)
📝 Ingredients (Serves 3–4):
Fresh karapincha (curry leaves) – 1 cup (loosely packed, washed)

Raw rice (white/red) – 1/4 cup

Grated coconut – 1/2 cup (or thick coconut milk – 1 cup)

Garlic – 2 cloves (crushed)

Small onions/shallots – 2 (sliced)

Cumin seeds – 1/2 tsp

Fenugreek seeds – 1/4 tsp (optional)

Water – 3–4 cups

Salt – to taste

🍳 Instructions:
Soak and cook the rice:

Wash and soak raw rice for about 15–30 minutes.

Add the rice, garlic, shallots, cumin seeds, and fenugreek seeds to a pot with 3 cups of water.

Cook until the rice is soft and slightly broken down. Add more water if needed.

Prepare the karapincha juice:

Blend washed curry leaves with 1/2 cup of water until smooth.

Strain through a fine sieve or cheesecloth to extract the green herbal juice. Set aside.

Add coconut milk:

If using grated coconut, blend with warm water and strain to extract thick coconut milk.

Add the coconut milk to the cooked rice porridge and stir gently.

Simmer for 2–3 minutes on low heat. Avoid boiling after coconut milk is added.

Add curry leaf extract:

Finally, add the karapincha juice to the porridge.

Stir well and let it warm gently for 1–2 minutes. Do not boil after adding the juice, to preserve nutrients and color.

Season and serve:

Add salt to taste.

Serve warm, optionally with a small piece of jaggery or a banana.
