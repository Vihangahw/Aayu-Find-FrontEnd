🌿 Kowakka Kenda Recipe
📝 Ingredients (for 3–4 servings):
Kowakka leaves/tender shoots – 1 cup (washed and finely chopped)

Raw rice (white or red) – 1/4 cup

Grated coconut – 1/2 cup (or thick coconut milk – 1 cup)

Garlic – 2 cloves (smashed)

Shallots or small onions – 2 (sliced)

Cumin seeds – 1/2 tsp

Fenugreek seeds – 1/4 tsp (optional)

Salt – to taste

Water – 3–4 cups

🍳 Instructions:
Wash and soak the rice for about 15–30 minutes to reduce cooking time.

Cook the rice:

In a clay pot or saucepan, add the rice with 3 cups of water.

Add garlic, shallots, cumin seeds, and fenugreek seeds (if using).

Boil on medium heat until the rice is soft and slightly mushy. Add more water if needed.

Add kowakka leaves:

Once rice is soft, add the finely chopped kowakka leaves.

Simmer for another 5–7 minutes until the leaves are cooked through but still bright green.

Add coconut:

If using grated coconut: Blend it with 1 cup warm water and strain to extract thick coconut milk.

Add this coconut milk to the porridge and stir well.

Simmer for just 2–3 minutes on low heat. Do not boil vigorously after adding coconut milk, to avoid curdling.

Season and serve:

Add salt to taste.

Serve warm, optionally with jaggery, a piece of bread, or a banana on the side.

🧑‍🍳 Tips:
You can blend the entire porridge (after cooking) for a smoother texture, but traditionally it is served with a coarse consistency.
