🌸 Heen Bovitiya Kenda (Herbal Porridge with Osbeckia octandra)
Heen Bovitiya (Osbeckia octandra) is a medicinal plant native to Sri Lanka, traditionally used to support liver health, regulate blood sugar, and aid digestion. The leaves are the most commonly used part for kenda.

📝 Ingredients (Serves 3–4):
Heen Bovitiya leaves – 1 cup (washed and chopped)

Raw rice (red or white) – 1/4 cup

Grated coconut – 1/2 cup (or thick coconut milk – 1 cup)

Garlic – 2 cloves (crushed)

Shallots (small onions) – 2 (sliced)

Cumin seeds – 1/2 tsp

Fenugreek seeds – 1/4 tsp (optional)

Water – 3–4 cups

Salt – to taste

🍳 Instructions:
Cook the rice:

Wash and soak the rice for 15–30 minutes.

In a clay pot or saucepan, add the rice with garlic, shallots, cumin, fenugreek, and 3 cups of water.

Boil until the rice becomes soft and starchy. Add water if needed to keep a porridge consistency.

Prepare Heen Bovitiya:

Option 1 (Traditional): Chop the leaves finely and add directly to the porridge.

Option 2 (Smooth): Blend the leaves with a little water and strain to extract a green juice.

Add Heen Bovitiya:

If using whole chopped leaves: Add them to the porridge once the rice is soft and cook gently for 5–7 minutes.

If using the juice: Add it after turning off the heat (or with very low heat) to preserve nutrients and color.

Add coconut milk:

Extract thick coconut milk from grated coconut or use canned.

Add to the pot and stir well. Let it heat gently for 2–3 minutes. Do not boil after this step.

Season:

Add salt to taste and stir to mix.

Serve:

Serve warm, optionally with a piece of jaggery, ripe banana, or coconut flatbread (pol roti).
